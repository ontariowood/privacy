# Privacy Policy for Image Detective

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Image Detective respects your privacy.

Image Detective uses your device's camera and photo library to allow you to capture or select images for identification.

Images may be processed as necessary to provide identification results. Image Detective does not sell your personal information.

Camera and photo library access are used only to provide the app's image identification features.

Image Detective does not use your images for advertising purposes.

Third-party services used to provide image identification may process images as necessary to return identification results.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the App Store listing.
